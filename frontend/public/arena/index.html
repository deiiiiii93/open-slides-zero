What the Arena Measures

The Arena is designed for financial-agent evaluation, not generic prompt scoring. Each trial drives live desk workflows, reconstructs the transcript from trace logs, and combines objective criteria with judge review.

  • Stateful, multi-step OTC derivatives workflows.
  • Headless operation with no human approval between steps.
  • Repeated trials to separate average ability from reliability.
  • Artifacts published as stable HTML, PDF, Markdown, and data files.
